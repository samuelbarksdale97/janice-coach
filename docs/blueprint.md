# **App Name**: Evolving Door

## Core Features:

- Home Page: Landing page with hero section, introduction, and calls to action.
- About Page: Detailed information about the coach's background, philosophy, and approach.
- Services Page: Clear presentation of service offerings with descriptions and calls to action.
- Blog Page: Regularly updated blog with articles and insights related to life coaching.
- Contact Page: Contact form and contact information for inquiries and booking consultations.
- Contact Form Submission with Firestore: Captures contact form submissions and stores them securely using Firestore, enabling easy management of leads and inquiries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Denim Blue (#31698A) for action elements like buttons and headlines, conveying professionalism and trustworthiness.
- Secondary color: Rose Pink (#E0A7B0) as an accent for sub-headlines and hover effects, adding warmth and approachability.
- Background color: White (#FFFFFF) for the main content areas, creating a clean and modern space.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a serif, for an elegant and fashionable look. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a sans-serif, combining a modern look with warmth. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Full-width sections with generous padding and clear visual hierarchy to ensure readability and engagement.
- Use professional and relevant icons for social media links and service cards.
